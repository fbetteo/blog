# About me

I’m Franco Betteo, an AI engineer and senior data scientist. I help teams turn a specific business problem into an AI or machine learning system they can evaluate, use, and maintain.

## Background

I have worked at Nielsen, InsiteAI, and Despegar, and with teams ranging from startups to the University of Oxford. Most of my work has been in retail and CPG: demand forecasting, assortment optimization, pricing analysis, segmentation, and product discovery.

For the University of Oxford, I developed educational materials for a machine learning program. At Diggity, I built the first MVP using probabilistic graphical models.

## What I build

Besides client work, I build and run my own products:

- [Download YouTube Transcripts](https://www.downloadyoutubetranscripts.com/): bulk transcript extraction from videos, channels, and playlists, with an API.
- [Sports Jobs Online](https://sportsjobs.online): a job board for sports analytics and sports industry roles.

Running products with real users keeps me close to the practical side of software: reliability, cost, and whether people actually use what was built.

## How I work

I work directly with your team and explain the tradeoffs behind each decision. I prefer starting with a clear question and a simple baseline, then adding complexity only when the evidence supports it. The goal is a system your team understands and can improve without me.

## Writing

I have been [writing about statistics and machine learning](writing/index.md) since 2018, first in Spanish and now mostly in English.
