# AI consulting for retail and CPG teams

I’m Franco Betteo, an AI engineer and senior data scientist. I help teams turn a specific business problem into an AI or machine learning system they can evaluate, use, and maintain.

My work spans demand forecasting, optimization, pricing analysis, segmentation, product discovery, and applied AI. I have worked at Nielsen, InsiteAI, and Despegar, and with teams ranging from startups to the University of Oxford.

[See selected projects](projects/index.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary } [Explore consulting services](services.md){ .md-button }

## Where I can help

- **Find a worthwhile use case.** Assess the data, expected value, risks, and a practical first experiment.
- **Build and evaluate a prototype.** Work with your team to test whether an approach solves the actual problem before planning production work.
- **Improve an existing system.** Review model quality, data pipelines, evaluation, and the path to reliable use.

I work directly with your team and explain the tradeoffs behind the solution. The goal is a system your team can understand and improve.

## Selected work

Much of my consulting work is for private clients, so I cannot share their data, code, or a public demo. Here are the kinds of decisions and workflows I have worked on:

- **Demand forecasting:** predicting future demand with gradient-boosted models, including LightGBM, so teams can plan with a view of likely demand.
- **Optimization:** using linear optimization with PuLP to compare feasible choices under business constraints.
- **Pricing and segmentation:** analyzing price elasticity and grouping customers or products with methods such as k-means and DBSCAN to support more targeted decisions.
- **Visual product discovery:** using image embeddings to find similar items in an ecommerce catalog.

The [projects page](projects/index.md) explains what I can share and how these methods relate to a buyer's problem.

### Product discovery for an ecommerce catalog

I built an image similarity system to help a retailer find related products in its catalog. The work included image collection, visual embeddings, a FAISS index, and an API for retrieving similar items. The project page explains the scope, implementation, and what is known about its use.

[Read the project](projects/ecommerce-image-similarity.md) · [Try the portfolio demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/fbetteo/fashion_similarity)

### A public product I build

[Download YouTube Transcripts](https://www.downloadyoutubetranscripts.com/) is a product I build and operate for extracting available transcripts from individual videos, channels, and playlists. It offers bulk processing and an API, including for teams preparing text for AI workflows.
