# Case study writing template

Keep this file outside `docs/` so unfinished case studies do not appear on the public site. Copy the sections below into `docs/projects/<descriptive-name>.md` when the facts and any client permissions are ready.

For confidential work, a useful case study can be anonymous and have no demo. Describe the industry and decision at a level you are allowed to share. Separate what you built from what the client later did, and avoid numerical impact claims without a baseline or permission to disclose them.

## Page metadata

Use a specific title and description in YAML front matter. State the business problem, industry, and solution in plain language. Avoid promising results that are not measured.

## Next step for a buyer

Explain which similar problem you can help with, the first evaluation step, and link to `../services.md` plus your contact option. Ask permission before naming clients, publishing screenshots, or quoting private results.

## Short version for a confidential project

If a full case study is not possible, use four short paragraphs on the projects page:

1. **Problem:** What decision or workflow needed improvement, without naming the client.
2. **Approach:** What you personally did and why the method fit the problem.
3. **Evidence:** What was delivered or evaluated, and any shareable result. Say when an outcome was not measured or cannot be disclosed.
4. **Relevance:** What a buyer with a similar problem should check first in their own data or process.
