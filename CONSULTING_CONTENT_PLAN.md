# Consulting content plan

## Baseline from Google Search Console

For the 28 settled days from 2026-08-17 through 2026-09-13, the site received most search clicks on educational posts: Poisson (10), Dirichlet (7), and weighted regression (7). The services page had 9 impressions and 0 clicks; the ecommerce image similarity article had 36 impressions and 1 click. These are small counts, so use them as a starting point rather than evidence of a trend.

## Next case studies to write

Use `CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md`. Publish only details you can verify and share.

1. **Demand forecasting client project.** Show the decision the forecast supported, the horizon, baseline, validation method, and business result if measured. Anonymize client details where needed.
2. **Optimization or pricing client project.** Explain the decision, constraints or elasticity assumptions, alternatives considered, and what was delivered. Distinguish an analytical recommendation from an implemented business change.
3. **Download YouTube Transcripts.** Explain the user problem, bulk processing workflow, API, operational constraints, and what you learned from running a public product.
4. **An advisory engagement.** Describe the initial decision, options assessed, recommendation, and what the client did next. This helps buyers understand an engagement that produces a decision rather than software.

## Articles for potential buyers

Write from first-hand project experience. Each article should answer a real question, show how you would evaluate the choice, and link to a relevant case study and service.

- How to test whether visual product search is useful for a retail catalog.
- What a retail AI prototype should prove before production work.
- How to evaluate a RAG application on a company’s own questions and documents.

Choose the article language based on the clients you want to reach. The existing Spanish statistics traffic does not by itself establish Spanish-language demand for consulting.

## Monthly measurement

- In Search Console: record clicks, impressions, queries, and average position for `/`, `/services/`, `/projects/`, and buyer-oriented articles. Separate branded, educational, and commercial queries.
- In analytics: measure visits from those pages to `/services/`, project pages, and booking or email clicks. Confirm the booking link leads to completed calls before treating clicks as leads.
- Record qualified enquiries and proposals manually, with the source when known. Review changes over several months because current commercial search volume is small.

Keep the old article URLs. Add contextual links only where the project or service is relevant to the article’s subject.
